There are many MiSTer FPGA cases to choose from: DIY project-box builds, retro-fitted old computer or console injection molded cases, 3D-printed designs, and manufactured aluminum heatsink cases.

## Get some ideas

If you want to make your own case, the ["Show us your MiSTer"](https://misterfpga.org/viewtopic.php?t=96){target=_blank} thread on the official MiSTer FPGA forums is a great source of ideas, with both store-bought and custom-built MiSTers on display.
