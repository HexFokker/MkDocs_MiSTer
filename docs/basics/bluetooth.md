The MiSTer supports Bluetooth adapters and Bluetooth-connected controllers.

## Compatible adapters

Generally speaking, most Bluetooth 4.0 adapters use one of two similar chips, the CSR8510 or the BCM20702, and both are compatible. Bluetooth 5.0 adapters are a little different, but many of them work as well. Known compatible adapters include:

* [Edimax BT-8500 (5.0)](https://www.amazon.com/Edimax-BT-8500-Bluetooth-Supports-Controllers/dp/B08M1VJHVD/){target=_blank}
* [MiSTerAddons.com's Bluetooth 4.2 / WiFi AC600 Combo Adapter](https://misteraddons.com/collections/parts/products/wifi-bt-usb-adapter){target=_blank}
* [MiSTerFPGA.co.uk's CSR8510 Bluetooth 4.0 Adapter](https://misterfpga.co.uk/product/mister-fpga-bluetooth-adapter-dongle/){target=_blank}
* [MiSTerKits.com's CSR8510 Bluetooth 4.0 Adapter](https://www.misterkits.com/products/mister-bluetooth-adapter){target=_blank}
* [ZEXMTE Long Range USB Bluetooth (5.0)](https://www.amazon.com/ZEXMTE-Bluetooth-Adapter-Keyboard-Headphones/dp/B09D7W918Q){target=_blank}

Many more adapters work, and most adapters seem to work fine. However, some hardware revisions may change compatibility even for the models listed here, so your results may vary.

## How to pair your Bluetooth controller or keyboard

1. Open the pairing prompt: press ++f11++ on a keyboard connected to the MiSTer, or, if you have a Digital I/O or Analog I/O board, press and hold its OSD button.
2. Put your controller into pairing mode and wait. The MiSTer should find it and connect within moments.
3. You may need to define the controller's inputs, even if you have used the controller over a cable before, because the MiSTer sees it as a different controller over Bluetooth.

## Bluetooth connection to MiSTer unstable?

If your Bluetooth connection is unstable (for example, the controller randomly disconnects), try a USB extension cable or pigtail adapter to move the Bluetooth adapter away from the USB hub. For some users, the USB hub can add interference and disrupt certain adapters, such as the ASUS USB-BT500 in some cases. The root cause is not currently known.
